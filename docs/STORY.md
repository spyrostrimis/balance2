# Balance — opening story

The premise and thematic direction below were approved on 8 November 2026.
The dialogue is a first writing pass. Character names, specific species,
settlement and planet names, and performance remain open.

## The dramatic promise

Build a future worth living in while confronting people who believe they alone
know how to save it. Blue's heroes can be flawed without abandoning compassion.
Red can be competent and sincere while its coercion remains consequential.
Different campaigns should reveal different experiences, not erase the harm done.

## Mission 01 — First Contact

**Location:** the Shattered Basin, near a frontier settlement.

**Immediate need:** a recent storm damaged the settlement's water system.
Korali is needed for replacement components. A freshly exposed deposit nearby
offers a chance to restore supply before the situation becomes desperate.

**Your expedition:** a Blue Captain and three soldiers. They are here for a
practical rescue effort, not to begin a planetary war.

**Opposition:** a Red patrol has orders to requisition the deposit. Its commander
offers protection and supplies if the settlement accepts permanent military
administration. This is an offer made under pressure, not a free choice.

**Blue's flaw:** distant leadership orders restraint and further negotiation,
but gives the people on the ground no timely way to repair the water system.
The Captain must own the consequences of acting.

## Opening sequence — draft

The camera follows a damaged water conduit away from the settlement road.
Wind carries sand across old paving. The expedition reaches the newly exposed ore.

**FIELD TECHNICIAN:** "The main intake is gone. We can patch the pipes, but the
regulator needs Korali. Without it, we're pumping sand."

**BLUE CAPTAIN:** "Then this deposit stays with the settlement."

The Red patrol arrives. Its commander surveys the damaged equipment before
addressing the Captain. The encounter is tense, not gleeful.

**RED COMMANDER:** "We have water carriers on the road. Food. Engineers.
Accept our administration and they'll be here before nightfall."

**BLUE CAPTAIN:** "For how long?"

**RED COMMANDER:** "For as long as order requires."

Blue command comes through in a clipped transmission.

**BLUE COMMAND:** "Do not escalate. The Council is reviewing the settlement's
status. Maintain your position until negotiations conclude."

**BLUE CAPTAIN:** "Send them the reservoir readings. Again."

The Red commander orders the expedition away from the ore. The Captain stays.
The player receives control. No branching dialogue or morality meter is needed.

## During the encounter — draft radio lines

**RED COMMANDER:** "We can keep these people alive. Your Council can keep meeting."

**BLUE CAPTAIN:** "Help them, then. Leave their government out of it."

**FIELD TECHNICIAN:** "There's paving under the ore. Older than the road on our charts."

Use sparingly, with space between lines. Tactical messages should not erase them.
No spoken voice performance or cutscene system is implemented yet.

## Victory

Securing the site is the first step toward repairing the water system; capture
does not magically perform extraction or complete the repair. The immediate
outcome is safe access for the follow-up survey and recovery team.

**BLUE CAPTAIN:** "Mark a route for the recovery crew. Water systems first."

A scanner follows Korali seams into worked stone beneath the deposit. The
stone bears the same divided-circle mark as the sealed monuments beyond it.

**FIELD TECHNICIAN:** "Captain. This isn't a natural fracture."

**BLUE CAPTAIN:** "Record it. Don't cut any deeper."

End with the monument still sealed. The mystery invites the next mission without
explaining the Clergy's history, motives, leader, or relationship to Korali.

The ending must also work if the Captain falls but the squad wins: surviving
troops report the discovery. Do not let a dead character speak in an outcome.

## Defeat

The Red patrol secures the site. The settlement's need remains unresolved by the
player's expedition. Show the local consequence without inventing a massacre or
pretending the player completed the rescue. Replay returns to the briefing.

## Questions reserved for later

- Who elected or appointed the Blue Captain, and what personal obligation keeps
  them here? Avoid immediately defaulting to a lost family or a chosen-one prophecy.
- Which Red beliefs were formed through real failures of civilian leadership?
- What do Green traders know about access to the basin and old survey records?
- Why does the Clergy restrict these monuments? Its explanation and the truth
  may differ, but neither is fixed yet.
- If Orichalcum is adopted, what unique narrative role does it serve? It is not
  automatically the explanation for every ancient mystery.
